Local Manufacturing Heritage

Rooted in Bengal's Textile Legacy

Historically, Bengal muslin represented the pinnacle of soft, featherweight luxury woven by master artisans. At TISHOO Manufacturing Company, we channeled this storied heritage into the modern hygiene space.

Based in Contai (Kanthi), East Medinipur, our modern manufacturing facility produces multi-purpose tissue napkins and facial paper designed to be gentle on delicate skin yet resilient against spills and grease.

From our illuminated showroom on Jatiya Vidyalaya Road to dining counters across the district, every single pull reflects our devotion to uncompromised quality.

Traditional Blessings
